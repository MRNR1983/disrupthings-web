In research

Companion for older adults

Agents that keep older or ill people company, connected to their watches and sensors, and that alert their family or caregivers.

The problem

Many older adults spend much of the day alone. Families learn late about what happens, and caregivers cannot be there all the time.

How it works

01

It talks with the person

It keeps them company, reminds them of their tasks and listens.

02

It connects to their devices

Smartwatches, sensors and other devices at home.

03

It alerts the people who matter

Family or caregivers get what they need, when they need it.

What it does

  • Daily company and conversation
  • Reminders for tasks and appointments
  • Readings from smartwatches and sensors
  • Alerts to family or caregivers

How we keep it safe

It handles health data, so it goes through a full legal review before launch.

We are not booking demos yet. We will show it once the review is done.
