Legal assistant for law firms

It answers, qualifies and books 24 hours a day. The lawyer gets every case summarized before the appointment.

The problem

Inquiries arrive Friday night, early Saturday or on Sunday, and nobody answers until Monday. By then the client has found another firm.

03

Works around the clock

It answers, qualifies the case, books the appointment and hands the lawyer a summarized case file.

What it does

  • First contact and case qualification, at any hour
  • Appointment booking with reminders
  • Client case file and documents kept in order
  • Draft filings for the lawyer to review
  • Deadlines and calculations: severance, alimony, procedural deadlines
  • Follow-up, retention and referrals after the case
  • Supporting research and firm reports

How we keep it safe

It introduces itself as the firm's assistant, never as a person. It only answers within each practice area; on criminal matters it informs and connects with the lawyer. A lawyer reviews every draft before it goes out.
