Flight claims

An agent that files your claim with the airline when your flight is delayed, your luggage is lost or the service is bad. It only charges if you recover something.

The problem

Almost nobody files a claim: the process is long, every airline asks for something different and it is unclear how much you are owed.

How it works

01

Tell us what happened

Upload your boarding pass and receipts.

02

The agent files the claim

It builds the claim with what each airline asks for, submits it and follows up.

03

It only charges if you win

If the airline pays, we keep a percentage of what is recovered.

What it does

  • Checks whether your case qualifies before starting
  • Builds the claim with the right documents
  • Submits the claim and follows up
  • Keeps you posted at every step

How we keep it safe

We are researching which airlines in Mexico pay without going to court, how much and how fast. The demo shows the full flow.
