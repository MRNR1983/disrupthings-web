Official degree by experience

For people who already know their job and want the official degree: diagnosis, study by topic and a mock exam, in one place.

The problem

In Mexico you can earn an official degree by proving what you already know, through the SEP's Acuerdo 286. But the information is scattered and there is no clear way to prepare for the exam.

How it works

01

Diagnosis

Before studying, you learn which topics you already master and which you do not.

02

Study by topic

A question bank per topic, written and reviewed in advance.

03

Mock exam

A full exam in real time, so there are no surprises.

What it does

  • Explains the process step by step, with its sources
  • Topic-by-topic diagnosis before you start
  • Question bank organized by topic
  • Full mock exam in real time
  • Helps you put together the admission file

How we keep it safe

Questions are written and reviewed in advance, never invented on the spot. Every fact about the process links to its official source.
