School agents

One action from the teacher becomes personalized follow-up for every family, through the channels they already use.

The problem

What happens in class rarely reaches home in full, and teachers have no time to write to every family. Before the school year starts, almost no one checks whether the chosen textbook covers the official curriculum.

How it works

01

The teacher teaches

They record the class from their phone. The transcript and notes are saved by group and by topic.

02

The system prepares the material

Plans, exams, rubrics and study guides, built from the curriculum and from what was covered in class. The teacher reviews and edits everything.

03

Each family gets what is theirs

One WhatsApp conversation per family, plus general announcements. Alerts arrive when something happens: an exam, a topic that was hard.

What it does

  • Checks whether the textbook covers the official curriculum and suggests alternatives if it does not
  • Builds the school-year plan, week by week
  • Generates exams and rubrics that the teacher reviews and edits
  • Prepares study guides and practice exams from class notes
  • Answers families' questions and hands the school anything that needs a person
  • Generates the school supply list and lets families buy it on WhatsApp, personalized

How we keep it safe

The teacher approves everything that reaches families. Practice material is labeled as practice, never as an official exam.
